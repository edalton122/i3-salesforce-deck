The Problem & The Solution

Today — Siloed Data

The same customer, contract, or employee appears differently across Deltek, the ATS, and SharePoint. Every cross-department handoff starts with reconciliation. Clearance data isn't tracked in any single place. Program status requires manual exports from three systems.

After MDM + Governance

Informatica MDM matches and merges records into one trusted golden record per entity. Survivorship rules decide which field wins — automatically. Data Quality flags clearance expiry 90 days out. Governance applies role-based privacy controls and produces the lineage documentation Infosec requires.
